# S2 both-off runs 80, 84–88, all services

600 s holds, both controllers off, `spawn_rate` 50. Paper-C1: frontend pinned at 4 replicas, every other service at 1. Sidecar request 100 m with no CPU limit. How the three signals are read: [2026-09-24-s2-both-off-abc-reading.md](2026-09-24-s2-both-off-abc-reading.md). The CPU table: [2026-09-26-s2-cpu-limits-for-spread.md](2026-09-26-s2-cpu-limits-for-spread.md).

Counts are getproduct / postcheckout / getcart / postcart / emptycart. run80 and run84 are 325 / 80 / 100 / 90 / 5. run85 and run86 do not store user counts; emptycart peaks at 5, getcart reaches 100, and postcart peaks near 90–95, the same plateau as run80. run87 is 275 / 70 / 100 / 90 / 5. run88 is 275 / 80 / 100 / 90 / 5, launched as a replay of run86. A bold bar separates each mix. Every number below is from `experiments/s2_both_off_abc.py` on these six folders, plus the Locust CSVs, `service_inbound.csv`, `resource_usage.csv`, and `topfull_detect.csv`.
