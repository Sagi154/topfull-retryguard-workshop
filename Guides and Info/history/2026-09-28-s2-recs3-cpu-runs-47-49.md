**200/250/200/50/50.** run47 clears streak 30 on 0 controlled services and an overloaded share of 0.5 on 0. Email is the hottest detector share, 35.0%. The largest retry edge is frontend → recommendationservice, 101,810. Pay-250 run44 on the same counts cleared the detector bar on email (83.3%) and the retry edge was frontend → recommendationservice, 119,133, with recommendations at 2 × 800 m.
