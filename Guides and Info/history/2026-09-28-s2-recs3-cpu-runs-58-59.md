# S2 both-off runs 58–59, all services

600 s holds, both controllers off, `spawn_rate` 50. Recs-3 CPU table: frontend 1050 m × 4, checkoutservice 800 m × 4, recommendationservice 700 m × 3, adservice 500 m × 1, and the other Pay-250 per-pod limits at 1 replica. Sidecar request **90 m** with no CPU limit. 360 s cool-off before run58 and between the holds. How the three signals are read: [2026-09-24-s2-both-off-abc-reading.md](2026-09-24-s2-both-off-abc-reading.md). The CPU table: [2026-09-26-s2-cpu-limits-for-spread.md](2026-09-26-s2-cpu-limits-for-spread.md).

Counts are getproduct / postcheckout / getcart / postcart / emptycart. The pulled run58 folder is the third launch of that slot. The first died on `duplicate session: throttle` before Locust. The second ran the timer and was cut by SIGTERM about 2.5 minutes in; that folder was overwritten by the relaunch. `service_capacity.json` matches Recs-3 on both holds. Locust `total.csv` has 562 rows on run58 and 516 on run59.

The replica-count gate still fails on paymentservice. run58 has `replica_count` 0 on 12 of 63 `resource_usage.csv` samples (CPU 248–252 m on 7 of those, 0 m on 5). run59 has it on 2 of 27 samples (one at 0 m, one at 250 m). Every other service stayed on the pin. The zero is `status.readyReplicas`: the payment readiness and liveness probes are `grpc_health_probe` with a 1 s timeout, so a pegged 250 m pod drops Ready while the process is still running.

## Per mix

**50/400/50/20/20.** run58 and run57 both fail the replica-count gate. Neither clears streak 30 on a controlled service. run58 clears an overloaded share of 0.5 on checkoutservice (585/639, 91.5%). run57's checkout share is 296/638 (46.4%). The retry edge on both is frontend → checkoutservice, 80,840 and 81,588. run57 also sends 10,545 retries into recommendationservice; run58 sends 0. postcheckout goodput is 1.3 and 1.7, with fail rate 0.976 and 0.974.

**25/500/25/20/20.** run59 fails the replica-count gate on two payment samples. It clears streak 30 on checkoutservice (31) and an overloaded share of 0.5 on checkoutservice (343/437, 78.5%). Payment's streak is 18. postcheckout goodput is 0.0 and its fail rate is 1.000. Payment arrival is 57.3 req/s, against 164.3 and 163.6 on the 50/400 mix. Frontend → checkoutservice retries are 56,301. Checkout → paymentservice retries are 468.
