# S2 both-off runs 71–73, all services

600 s holds, both controllers off, `spawn_rate` 50, Istio `attempts: 3`, `per_try_timeout_ms: 500`. 360 s cool-off before run71 and between holds. Runs 71–73 are the browse holds. How the three signals are read: [2026-09-24-s2-both-off-abc-reading.md](2026-09-24-s2-both-off-abc-reading.md). The CPU tables: [2026-09-26-s2-cpu-limits-for-spread.md](2026-09-26-s2-cpu-limits-for-spread.md). Layout: [2026-09-27-s2-cpu-variant-handoff.md](2026-09-27-s2-cpu-variant-handoff.md) §Post-run analysis. Final-candidate holds run60–run70: [2026-09-29-s2-runs-60-70.md](2026-09-29-s2-runs-60-70.md).

Counts are getproduct / postcheckout / getcart / postcart / emptycart. (a)/(b)/(c) and CPU mean / max are from `experiments/s2_both_off_abc.py`. The other tables use the same Locust, inbound, CPU, and detector reads as [2026-09-27-s2-checkout3-cpu-runs-39-42.md](2026-09-27-s2-checkout3-cpu-runs-39-42.md). Sidecar CPU limit was unset. run71 used sidecar request 100 m. run72 and run73 used 90 m.
