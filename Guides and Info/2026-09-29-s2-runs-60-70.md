# S2 both-off runs 60–70, all services

600 s holds, both controllers off, `spawn_rate` 50, Istio `attempts: 3`, `per_try_timeout_ms: 500`. 360 s cool-off before run60 and between holds. Runs 60–70 are the 2026-09-29 final-candidate series. run6, run42, and run43 stay in the comparison columns as the earlier hold of that mix. How the three signals are read: [2026-09-24-s2-both-off-abc-reading.md](2026-09-24-s2-both-off-abc-reading.md). The CPU tables: [2026-09-26-s2-cpu-limits-for-spread.md](2026-09-26-s2-cpu-limits-for-spread.md). Layout: [2026-09-27-s2-cpu-variant-handoff.md](2026-09-27-s2-cpu-variant-handoff.md) §Post-run analysis. Browse holds run71–run73: [2026-09-29-s2-runs-71-73.md](2026-09-29-s2-runs-71-73.md).

Counts are getproduct / postcheckout / getcart / postcart / emptycart. (a)/(b)/(c) and CPU mean / max are from `experiments/s2_both_off_abc.py`. The other tables use the same Locust, inbound, CPU, and detector reads as [2026-09-27-s2-checkout3-cpu-runs-39-42.md](2026-09-27-s2-checkout3-cpu-runs-39-42.md). Sidecar CPU limit was unset. Paper holds used sidecar request 90 m. The other holds in this file used 100 m.
